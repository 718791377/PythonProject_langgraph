# Legal Contract Review Record

## Purpose

Records actual legal and contract review. This file is a fillable record for one operational instance, not a third white paper and not a regenerated 48/60 operations package.

## Authority Boundary

The one-person company owns business truth for customers, revenue, pricing, contracts, legal posture, cashflow, support, refunds, delivery commitments, and business learning. Codex execution truth remains protected by Agent permissions, runtime validation, durable-state recovery, local-main evidence, rollback gates, and remote-publication gates.

Canonical authority tokens: business truth; customers; revenue; pricing; contracts; legal posture; cashflow; support; refunds; delivery commitments; business learning; must not bypass Agent permissions; runtime validation; durable-state recovery; local-main evidence; rollback gates; remote-publication gates.

## Inputs

- Customer, revenue, pricing, contract, legal, cashflow, support, refund, delivery, or learning evidence.
- Linked Codex execution records when business decisions require code, Agent, or runtime changes.
- Human review context for non-delegable commitments.

## Tools And Techniques

- Business-truth review across customers, revenue, pricing, contracts, legal posture, cashflow, support, refunds, delivery commitments, and business learning.
- Tailoring decision review for full, whole-project lightweight, phase-local lightweight, module-level lightweight, or hybrid modes.
- Dual-gate escalation when business outcomes need Codex execution work.

## Outputs

- A business operating record with evidence, owner, decision, and next action.
- A linked mixed-governance or Codex intake when runtime work is needed.
- A human-confirmed business commitment that must not bypass Agent permissions, runtime validation, durable-state recovery, local-main evidence, rollback gates, or remote-publication gates.

## Fillable Record

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |

| linked_docs | paths |

## Review Notes

- Record only evidence that belongs to this instance.
- Link source documents instead of copying unrelated truth into this file.
- Escalate mixed items to the mixed-governance instance pack before commitments or remote-publication actions.
