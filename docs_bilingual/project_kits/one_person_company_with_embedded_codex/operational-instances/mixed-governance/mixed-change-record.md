# Mixed Change Record

## Purpose

Records a real mixed business and Codex change. This file is a fillable record for one operational instance, not a third white paper and not a regenerated 48/60 operations package.

## Authority Boundary

Mixed governance requires a business gate, a Codex execution gate, and human final approval whenever a change affects customer, revenue, or compliance commitments and code, Agent, or runtime behavior. Human final approval is required for pricing, contracts, release, customer promises, financial commitments, legal commitments, and remote publication.

Canonical authority tokens: business gate; Codex execution gate; human final approval; pricing; contracts; release; customer promises; financial commitments; legal commitments; remote publication.

## Inputs

- A change or review item that affects customer, revenue, or compliance commitments and code, Agent, or runtime behavior.
- Business evidence plus Codex execution evidence.
- Tailoring mode, risk status, and human review context.

## Tools And Techniques

- Dual-gate review with a business gate and a Codex execution gate.
- Impact mapping across pricing, contracts, release, customer promises, financial commitments, legal commitments, and remote publication.
- Human final approval before any external commitment or remote-publication action.

## Outputs

- A mixed-governance record with both gates, evidence, decisions, and unresolved risks.
- A release, intake, impact, tailoring, or portfolio review record ready for audit.
- A human-approved or blocked decision with linked business and Codex artifacts.

## Fillable Record

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| business gate | passed / blocked / not applicable |
| Codex execution gate | passed / blocked / not applicable |
| human final approval | approved / rejected / pending |
| affected_commitments | pricing / contracts / release / customer promises / financial commitments / legal commitments / remote publication |
| linked_docs | paths |

## Review Notes

- Record only evidence that belongs to this instance.
- Link source documents instead of copying unrelated truth into this file.
- Escalate mixed items to the mixed-governance instance pack before commitments or remote-publication actions.
