# Change: Synthesize PMI And Business Sources Into Lifecycle Governance

## Why

The product lifecycle documents now need a stronger architecture-model refresh. The Codex CLI personal production durable-team target must be expressed as a 12-layer trunk with 4 virtual departments, 24 responsibility domains, 18 cross-cutting modules, 48 core documents, 12 governed AI agents, and 12 recommended books. The one-person-company operating-system target must be expressed as a 12-layer trunk with 5 virtual departments, 30 responsibility domains, 20 cross-cutting modules, 60 core documents, 15 governed AI agents, 15 recommended books, and 12 minimal startup documents.

The local `Business Management/` and `PMP/` libraries have been extracted at PDF page, EPUB spine-item, and Markdown full-text granularity. The repository should record the resulting governance model without committing raw books or reproducing copyrighted source text.

## What Changes

- Update product lifecycle references with explicit two-target architecture inventories.
- Add process-group style `Inputs / Tools and Techniques / Outputs` guidance across lifecycle layers and execution-facing files.
- Add source-reading coverage summaries for Business Management and PMP sources.
- Update the Codex CLI durable-team white paper, MRD, and North Star/implementation plan so they align with the 12-layer architecture and PMI delivery overlay.
- Add one-person-company operating-system lifecycle guidance and minimal startup-document guidance for downstream projects.

## Impact

- Documentation-only change under `docs_bilingual/` and the product-lifecycle OpenSpec spec.
- No runtime behavior change.
- Local source extraction artifacts remain ignored under `.local/` and are not committed.
